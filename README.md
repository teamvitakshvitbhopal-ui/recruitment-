# Team VITAKSH — Avionics Recruitment

## VEGA-01 | Flight Systems Challenge

> **The payload is ready. The ground station isn't. And somewhere in the telemetry, something is wrong.**

Welcome to the avionics challenge for Team VITAKSH.

These two practical missions are built around a small high-altitude balloon called **VEGA-01**. You will work with telemetry, ground systems, electronics, embedded software, and fault investigation.

This is not a theory exam. Documentation, datasheets, search, compilers, CAD tools, GitHub, and AI tools may be used. The important part is that you understand and can explain what you submit.

## 🛰️ Mission 01 — Wake the Ground Station

Turn a raw telemetry stream into a ground-station view that a flight operator could actually use.

→ [Open Mission 01](mission-01-ground-station/task.md)

## What we want to see

We do not expect you to know everything already.

We are interested in how you:

**learn → build → test → break → debug → explain**

A simple solution that was tested and understood is more useful than a complicated solution that was copied and never checked.

## Suggested schedule

You have roughly **two weeks** for the complete challenge. Use the time to build a first version, test it, investigate, improve, and document.

## Submission

Keep your work under `submission/` and use the provided submission template.
